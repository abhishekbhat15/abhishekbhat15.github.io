Motivation

I like finding out why a material, a cell or a process behaves the way it does. Running cycling and EIS tests on Li-ion cells, then tracing a deviation back to its cause with SEM/EDX and failure analysis, is the work I enjoy most.

My path runs from automotive manufacturing, through an EV battery and fast-charging company, to a master’s in electrochemical and energy materials. I want to keep working where materials, processes and data meet, in process engineering, manufacturing or R&D for energy and industrial technology.

Selected projects

Exponent Energy · 2022–2024

Cell testing and joint quality

The result went into the component specification with design and quality teams.

Exponent Energy · 2022–2024

Process development and failure analysis

Identified 3 critical process–material interactions behind porosity, spatter and cracking with SEM/EDX and implemented corrective actions. A DoE with 12+ parameter sets raised first-pass yield from 80% to 97% and cut scrap by about 40%. The process was documented in SOPs for pre-series production at 500 units/week.

Mindset

Find the root cause

A deviation is a clue. I follow the data, from test curves to micrographs, until the cause is clear.

Test in a structured way

Planned experiments beat trial and error. A well-designed DoE got a process from 80% to 97% yield.

Make it repeatable

If others cannot reproduce a result, it is not finished. I write SOPs, protocols and clear reports.

Care for the data

Careful protocols and clean data are the base for every conclusion I draw.
